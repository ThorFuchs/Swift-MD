Reproducible Analysis of a Small Dataset

Illustrative methods paper · synthetic observations

Abstract

A transparent analysis records where data came from, how it was transformed, and which results were produced. This fictional example demonstrates that workflow with invented measurements.

Dataset and calculation

For each observation, the change from baseline is Δi=yi−bi\Delta_i = y_i - b_i. The average change across nn observations is:

Δ‾=1n∑i=1n(yi−bi)\overline{\Delta} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}(y_i-b_i)
Sample Baseline Follow-up Change
S1 12 15 +3
S2 10 12 +2
S3 14 18 +4 
let changes = [3.0, 2.0, 4.0]
let average = changes.reduce(0, +) / Double(changes.count)
print(average) // 3.0

Interpretation

The average change is 3 units in this synthetic dataset. This is a worked example, not evidence of an effect; real analysis requires appropriate data, uncertainty estimates and a study design suited to the research question.
