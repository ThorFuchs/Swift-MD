A Tiny Study of Indoor Light

Illustrative research paper · fictional data

Abstract

This small example explores how a workstation's distance from a window might relate to measured illuminance. The measurements below are invented to illustrate a reproducible Markdown document, not a scientific finding.

Methods

We compare three imagined positions using the mean of repeated readings. For readings x1,…,xnx_1, \ldots, x_n, the sample mean is:

func mean(_ readings: [Double]) -> Double {
    readings.reduce(0, +) / Double(readings.count)
}

Discussion

In this fictional example the mean decreases with distance. A real study would need a calibrated sensor, repeated days, controlled weather and uncertainty estimates before drawing conclusions.
